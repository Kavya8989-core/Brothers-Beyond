🕹️ Brothers Beyond

«A story-driven 2D adventure about brotherhood, sacrifice, and finding your way back home.»

Brothers Beyond is an indie 2D adventure game currently being developed in Godot.

The game follows two brothers who are pulled into a mysterious world after a seemingly harmless video game turns into something far more dangerous.

---

📖 Story

The story begins with two brothers.

The older brother works hard to support his younger brother and eventually gifts him a video game. But the game isn't what it seems.

Inside the game, a mysterious wizard tells the younger brother that his older brother has been trapped.

Believing him, the younger brother enters the portal to rescue his brother.

But the wizard is lying.

The wizard's real goal is to escape his own prison by trapping others inside the world.

What begins as a simple rescue mission slowly turns into a journey through dangerous environments, mysterious characters, puzzles, enemies, and difficult choices.

And eventually, the brothers will have to find their way back home.

---

🎮 Gameplay

Brothers Beyond is being designed around:

- 🗺️ Exploration
- ⚔️ Combat
- 🧩 Puzzles
- 💬 NPC interactions
- 📜 Story-driven progression
- 🌎 Multiple environments
- 🤝 Brother-focused gameplay mechanics

The game will gradually introduce new mechanics and challenges as the story progresses.
